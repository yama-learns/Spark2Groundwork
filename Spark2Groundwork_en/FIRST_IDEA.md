Your task: split the idea below into conjectures that can be tested independently.

⛔ Do not search the literature this round.
⛔ Do not evaluate whether the idea is good.
Do exactly one thing: decompose.

===============================================================
Decomposition rules
===============================================================

1. Every conjecture must be **readable on its own** — no dependence on context
   or on the other entries.

2. Each one needs the fields below. **If you cannot fill one, say so. Do not invent.**

   Statement:        one sentence
   Origin:           which sentence or paragraph of the original
   Falsification:    which **observable result** would refute it
   Strongest rival:  name a specific existing position.
                     ⛔ Not "some scholars disagree"
   Rival's differing prediction: on **which observation** would the two differ

3. ⛔ If you cannot give a falsification condition, **do not force one**.
   **Put "-" in the falsification field** and add a following line
   `**Why I could not fill this:** ...`

   ⛔ Do not write "pending", "see below", or similar in the falsification field.
   ⚠️ **Reason (measured in this framework): that wording makes the sensor treat the field
   as filled, and the warning disappears silently** — the count dropped and the exit code
   stayed at 0.

   ℹ️ **Writing "-" is not worse than inventing.** The sensor's warning condition is
   "not yet adjudicated by a human", **not "field is empty"** — filling in text does not
   clear the warning.

4. ⛔ If you cannot answer field five, then field four is not a competing account,
   only a rephrasing.
   **A decorative rival is worse than none**, because it makes people believe the
   critical thinking has already been done.

5. After decomposing, also answer:

   - Which sentence in the original is **least falsifiable**? Why?
   - Which sentence do you think is **most valuable and the author may not have noticed**?
   - Are any two conjectures actually the same one?
   - Does any one conjecture actually contain three or four?

===============================================================
Output format
===============================================================

List the conjectures first (numbered from C-01), then answer the four questions in point 5.

⛔ Do not propose a research design this round.
⛔ Do not judge which direction is feasible this round.
**If the decomposition is wrong, everything after it is evidence for the wrong thing.**
