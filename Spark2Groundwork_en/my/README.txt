my/ -- your things live here

Everything in this folder is yours. ⛔ A framework upgrade never touches it.

  MY_RULES.md          your working rules. The framework's rules are copied into §1;
                       your own rules go in §2.
  MY_INCIDENTS.md      what actually happened in this project, and your own failure families.
  MY_INDEX.md          your file index. ⛔ Generated by a program; do not edit by hand.
  MY_INDEX_notes.json  the index's description column. Edit it, or have an AI keep it.
  tools/               scripts you wrote yourself.

To update the index, run:
    python scripts/harness/tool_my_index.py

⚠️ The index holds everything the framework does not own -- not a list of what to include.
   So any folder you invent appears in it automatically; you never register anything.

⛔ Why tools/ lives here and not in scripts/:
   scripts/ is a framework folder and is replaced wholesale on upgrade.
   A script of your own placed in scripts/ disappears at the next upgrade,
   and nothing tells you it happened.

⚠️ This note is a .txt, not a .md, for the same reason as in handoffs/:
   the .md files in this folder have their own checkers, and a note would fail them.
